Chapter 2 · The lazy star rule

"Longer = more helpful" → 98% of Pip's answers became the gushing paragraph.

With a leash, real quality goes from −0.34 (no leash) to +0.13 and +0.31 (stronger leashes).

A busy team rewards length. Coaching runs straight to the longest, gushing answer. The leash (a KL penalty) makes Pip lose stars for drifting far from how it answered before; learners slide it and watch real quality recover.
