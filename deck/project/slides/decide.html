Chapter 5 · The decision game

Prompt, fine-tune, or coach?

📎

Missing facts?

Give them in the prompt or via search. Never RL.

🛒

Tried a good ready-made chatbot?

Do that first: it's already coached. (Ours: 52% vs our 65%.)

👀

Wrong format or tone?

Fine-tune with LoRA on good examples.

⭐

Picks the worse of two OK answers?

Coach it, only with lots of comparisons (or an automatic checker), budget, and upkeep.

Learners play four scenarios in the notebook (new products, JSON invoices, maths tutor, Acme), then the checklist. Coaching is the last tool, not the first.
