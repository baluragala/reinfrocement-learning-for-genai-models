The KL leash: maximise reward − β · KL

β = 0

No leash. 100% on the long, gushing reply the naive reward loves. Acme's real reward: −0.34.

β = 1

Partly leashed. 43% gushing, the rest near the start. Real reward: +0.13.

β = 3

Stays near the reference, where the model already did well. Real reward: +0.31.

Naive reward = words ÷ 30 ("longer is more helpful"); real reward = Acme's rule check, never used for training. Reference run (pre-run notebooks in reference_runs/).

Why the tuned model is kept close to its starting point. The reward is changed to reward minus beta times the KL divergence from the reference, the SFT model. Rewards are imperfect proxies: here a busy team rewards length. With no leash the policy runs to the reply the proxy loves and real quality collapses. A larger beta keeps changes small and safe. Beta is a safety setting, not just a tuning knob. The trained RL adapter used beta 0.1 in DPO's scale.
