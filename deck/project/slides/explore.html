Exploit what you know, or explore?

0%

ε = 0 (never explores): found the best banner in 0% of runs. It locks onto its first lucky guess.

Most clicks

ε = 0.1: explores 10% of the time and ends with the most clicks overall.

93%

ε = 0.5 finds the best banner in 93% of runs, but keeps wasting half its impressions.

Banner recommender, 5 banners with hidden click rates, 30 runs × 3,000 impressions. Reference run (pre-run notebooks in reference_runs/).

The recommender picks one of five banners; click rates are hidden. Epsilon-greedy: with probability epsilon show a random banner, otherwise the best so far. Zero exploration gets stuck on whatever looked good first. Too much exploration keeps paying for knowledge it already has. The best setting is in between. Link forward: when an LLM samples at temperature above zero during RL training, that's exploration over replies.
