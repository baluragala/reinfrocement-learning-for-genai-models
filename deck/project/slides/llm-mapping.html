One difference matters: for an LLM, someone has to build the reward: a rule, a trained reward model, or people.

Map the vocabulary. The model is the policy, the prompt is the state, the reply is the action, and the reward is a score given once the reply is finished. The one real difference from the grid: there, the environment was the reward. For an LLM somebody builds it, and everything in this section is about what that choice does.
