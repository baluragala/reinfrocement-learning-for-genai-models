SFT copies the mix. It can't rank.

54%

of policy replies in the transcripts are good. SFT reproduces 54%.

23%

23%

To prefer good over better, the model needs a signal that ranks replies: a reward.

Style mix of the 149 policy-question transcripts the LoRA adapter was trained on.

The hinge of the session. Supervised fine-tuning maximises the likelihood of every training reply. In the limit it reproduces the data's mix, flaws included. It can say 'be like this example' but never 'this example is better than that one'. That is the gap RL fills, and the rest of the session is about how.
