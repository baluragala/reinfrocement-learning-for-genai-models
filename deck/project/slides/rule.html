The one rule

You can't tell Pip what to do. You can only hand out ⭐ stars.

That rule is reinforcement learning: learning from rewards for your own tries, not from right answers.

Say the rule, then let it sink in. In ordinary machine learning we show the model the right answer. Here we can't. We can only score what Pip tried. That's the whole difference, and it's how chatbots get their judgement.
