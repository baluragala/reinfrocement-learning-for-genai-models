Chapter 1 · Copying vs earning stars

Copy a teacher, or learn from stars?

📋

Copy-cat Pip

copies Sam, who usually grabs the coin → goes to the coin

⭐

Star Pip

never saw Sam, only stars → reaches the goal

Copying = supervised learning: only as good as the teacher. Stars = RL: can beat the teacher, if the stars are right.

This contrast maps onto chatbots later: fine-tuning copies example answers; reinforcement learning scores the model's own answers.
