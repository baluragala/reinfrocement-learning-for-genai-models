Chapter 1 · The gold-star trap

The race car circled the ⚡ turbo pad 40 times and never finished.

It did exactly what the stars paid for. The learning worked; the stars were the bug. Learners fix it by changing two numbers.

Reward hacking. The designer paid 1 star per pad visit to encourage speed; circling beats finishing. Same thing happened in OpenAI's CoastRunners boat game. In the challenge learners change only the star values until they see the trophy.
