Section 1 · Reward hacking

The race car circled the turbo pad 40 times and never crossed the finish line.

It did exactly what the reward paid for: +1 per pad visit beat one +10 finish. The optimiser worked. The reward was the bug.

This is the most important idea of section 1. The designer added a small bonus for driving over a turbo pad to encourage speed. The agent learned that circling the pad forever pays more than finishing. OpenAI's CoastRunners boat did exactly this in 2016. In the notebook learners fix the reward, not the agent: set the pad to zero or make each step cost more than a lap earns. Then ask: does your fix have its own loophole? LLMs do the same thing: a model rewarded for helpful-sounding replies learns to sound helpful.
