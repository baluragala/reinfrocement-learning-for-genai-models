Preference check on 24 fresh pairs not used in training. Reference run (pre-run notebooks in reference_runs/).

This is the conceptual heart of section 4. LoRA changed how the model talks: Acme's voice, formats and facts, plus the flaws of its transcripts. RL changed which reply it prefers. Log-probabilities on fresh pairs measure that directly: LoRA prefers the chosen reply 58% of the time, RL 79%. Interesting honest detail: on pushback pairs RL mostly did not adopt the raters' agreement habit in this run.
