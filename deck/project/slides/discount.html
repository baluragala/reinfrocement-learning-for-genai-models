Agents optimise long-term reward

γ = 0.3: short-sighted

Walks 3 steps to the +2 coin.

+10 that's 10 steps away is worth 10 × 0.3⁹ ≈ 0.0002 today.

γ = 0.95: far-sighted

Walks past the coin, 10 steps to the +10 goal.

The same +10 is worth 10 × 0.95⁹ ≈ 6.3 today.

Same grid, same rewards, same algorithm (Q-learning). Only the discount γ changed.

Q-learning on a 4×8 grid with walls, −0.1 per step, 2,000 episodes. Reference run (pre-run notebooks in reference_runs/).

The discount gamma says how much a reward one step later is worth compared with now. Same world, two gammas, two different destinations. Point at the maths on the cards: a far reward is multiplied by gamma once per step of delay. This is why RL agents give up small immediate rewards for larger later ones.
