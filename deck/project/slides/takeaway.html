The one takeaway

RL changes which of its possible replies a model prefers. It doesn't teach it anything new.

So it's only as good as the preferences you give it, and only worth it when prompting and LoRA can't close a judgement gap you can measure on your own prompts.

Say it once, slowly. Then the recap: pretraining gives knowledge, SFT and LoRA give format and style, RL gives judgement. RLHF gets there through a reward model and PPO, DPO directly from pairs, and both inherit the raters' habits. In Acme's comparison, RL fixed over-refusal and dishonesty, cost a little accuracy through over-hedging, and trained harder it got worse. Nobody learned a fact that wasn't in the data.
