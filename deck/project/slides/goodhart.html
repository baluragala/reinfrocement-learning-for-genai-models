Miniature policy optimised against Notebook 03's reward model. The same shape as Gao et al. (2022) on real reward models.

Goodhart's law as a curve. Optimise harder and harder, smaller beta, against the learned reward model. The proxy rises monotonically. The gold measure rises first, from real improvements, peaks at beta 1, then collapses as the policy discovers a rare padded, agreeable reply the reward model over-scores. Defences: sensible beta, early stopping, varied reward-model data, ensembles, and a held-out evaluation the optimiser never trains against. Then point back to rl_long: the same shape, on a real model.
