Ask the room first: which feature gets the biggest positive weight? Then reveal. Admitting uncertainty and valid JSON are rewarded, as intended. But the top two are agreeing with the customer and length. Nobody wrote a rule saying agree with customers. The raters preferred agreeable replies when customers pushed back, and gushing replies to complaints, and the reward model learned those habits as general rules. Whatever the reward model rewards, RL will amplify. In the notebook, RLHF on the miniature policy improves six kinds of message and turns the seventh, pushback, sycophantic.
