Rule-based checks

Cheap, repeatable, readable.

Blind to anything they don't test: a reply in the wrong language can pass.

Reads everything, scales.

85% of verdicts flipped when only the order of the two replies changed. Every consistent verdict went to the longer reply.

Three graders. Rules are cheap and blind. The judge reads everything but has biases. Ask the room to predict how often it flips when only the order changes, then reveal 85%. Position bias this strong means a single-order judge is close to a coin flip weighted by position. The judge also favours length, the same habit the raters had.
