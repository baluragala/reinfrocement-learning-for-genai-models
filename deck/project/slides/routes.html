Two routes from the same comparisons

Preference pairs
prompt, chosen, rejected

RLHF

① Train a reward model: score(chosen) > score(rejected)

② RL (PPO): sample replies, score with the reward model, nudge, minus β·KL

The same pairs

DPO

One step: raise log P(chosen), lower log P(rejected), both relative to the reference, scaled by β.

No reward model, and no sampling.

Both aim at the same target: π* ∝ π_ref · exp(reward / β). Intuition only; no derivations today.

RLHF is three stages: SFT, then a reward model trained on the comparisons, then RL against that reward model with a KL leash. DPO notices that the best policy for RLHF's objective can be written in terms of the policy itself, so it trains straight on the pairs: make chosen more likely and rejected less likely, relative to the reference model. Same data, same beta, different machinery. Keep it at this level; the session plan asks for intuition only.
