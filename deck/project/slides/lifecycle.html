Chapter 1 · From robots to chatbots

Every chatbot grows up in three stages

📚

School

pretraining
reads the internet → knowledge

→

👀

Shadowing

fine-tuning (LoRA)
copies examples → Acme's format & voice

→

⭐

Coaching

reinforcement learning
scored answers → judgement

Real models: Qwen2.5-0.5B (school), + LoRA on 480 Acme transcripts (shadowing), + DPO on 480 comparisons (coaching).

School-Pip knows where the Eiffel Tower is but invents Acme's policies ('free shipping over $10'). Shadowing-Pip answers like an Acme agent. But shadowing copies all the examples, good and rambling, which motivates coaching.
