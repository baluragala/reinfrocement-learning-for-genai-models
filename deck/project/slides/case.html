The running case

Acme wants its own small support model

What Acme has

~480 historical replies written by support agents: mostly good, some rambling, some that ignore the customer's format, a few that should never have been sent

~480 comparisons: a support lead read two replies and picked the better one

Its rules: 30-day returns · final sale and gift cards never refundable · free shipping over $75 · never share customer data

Five versions of one model

base: Qwen2.5-0.5B, pretrained only

lora: + LoRA fine-tuning on the replies

rl: + DPO on the comparisons

rl_long: the same DPO, trained harder

instruct: the vendor's own RL-tuned model, just prompted

All open models, run locally. Judge: Qwen2.5-1.5B-Instruct. No API key needed.

Acme Outfitters again. They want a small self-hosted model for cost and privacy. Their assets are exactly what real teams have: historical replies of mixed quality, and some comparisons from senior staff. The data is generated from a small knowledge base so every fact and flaw is traceable, and two rater biases were planted on purpose: preferring gushing replies to complaints, and agreeing with customers who confidently state a wrong policy. The model results, though, are real. The five variants share one base model, one prompt format and greedy decoding, so every difference comes from tuning.
